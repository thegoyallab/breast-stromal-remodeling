# Data access and redistribution

This software release does not redistribute primary third-party human datasets.

Primary data used by the manuscript were obtained from published public repositories or, where applicable, controlled-access resources. The exact accessions, cohort provenance, patient-count adjudication and assay definitions are documented in the manuscript, Supplementary Tables and the accompanying resource catalogue.

## Controlled or restricted data

Users are responsible for obtaining any controlled-access datasets under the original repository's authorization and data-use terms.

## Derived data

Small manuscript-level summary and audit tables are retained when needed to verify reported results. Two large cell-level derived mapping exports from FELINE and BREAKFAST are intentionally omitted from the public software archive. They can be regenerated from the cited public datasets.

No raw patient-identifiable data, authentication credentials or access tokens are intentionally included in this repository.
